Common questions

How do I know if my panel needs upgrading?

Signs include flickering lights, frequent breaker trips, or an old fuse box. We offer free inspections to assess your panel's condition.

Do you offer warranties on residential work?

Yes, all our work comes with a warranty on labor and parts. Details vary by project.
