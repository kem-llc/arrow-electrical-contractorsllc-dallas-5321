Our story

Your Trusted Dallas Electrician Since 1998

To provide safe, reliable, and affordable electrical services that exceed customer expectations, while maintaining the highest standards of professionalism and integrity.

Arrow Electrical Contractors was founded in 1998 by master electrician John Arrow with a simple mission: deliver top-quality electrical work with honesty and transparency. Starting with a single truck and a commitment to excellence, John built a reputation for reliability that spread through Dallas neighborhoods. Over the years, Arrow Electrical has grown into a full-service electrical company serving thousands of residential and commercial clients.

Today, our team of licensed electricians continues that tradition. We invest in ongoing training, use the latest tools and technology, and prioritize customer satisfaction above all. Whether it's a small repair or a major installation, we treat every project with the same care and attention to detail. Arrow Electrical — proud to be Dallas's trusted electrician.
